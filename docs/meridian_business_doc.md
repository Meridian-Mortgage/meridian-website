# Meridian — Business Document

## What We Do

Meridian is a branding and web design agency built exclusively for mortgage professionals. We serve solo loan officers, mortgage brokers, and brokerages across North America.

We build two things: brands that command authority, and websites that generate leads. Every decision we make is tied to one outcome — making our clients the most credible, most visible mortgage professional in their market.

---

## Services

### 1. Web Design — Primary Service

We build custom websites for mortgage professionals. Not templates. Not page builders. Purpose-built, conversion-focused sites designed to make homebuyers and referral partners trust the broker before a conversation starts.

**What the best broker websites do:**
- Establish instant credibility — the visitor knows within 3 seconds they are dealing with a professional
- Convert visitors into leads — clear CTAs, frictionless contact, integrated intake forms
- Build trust through social proof — testimonials, results, case studies
- Rank on Google — SEO fundamentals built in from the start
- Work on every device — mobile-first, fast-loading
- Connect to their CRM — GoHighLevel integration standard

**What we include:**
- Custom design — every site is built for that broker specifically
- Full copywriting direction
- Mobile and desktop optimised
- GoHighLevel integration
- Lead capture and contact forms
- SEO foundation
- Deployment and handover

**Who it is for:**
- Solo loan officers who want to stand out in a crowded market
- Mortgage brokers building a personal brand
- Brokerages that want a flagship web presence

---

### 2. Brand Identity — Secondary Service

Before a broker can show up online, they need to know what they look like. Meridian builds visual identities that communicate authority and trust — the foundation that makes everything else, including the website, work harder.

**What we include:**
- Logo and wordmark
- Colour palette
- Typography system
- Brand guidelines document
- Basic asset kit for social and print

**Who it is for:**
- Brokers launching or relaunching their personal brand
- Loan officers who have a website but no consistent visual identity
- Brokerages that want a cohesive look across their team

## Target Client

**Primary:** Solo loan officers and independent mortgage brokers
- High income, competitive, results-driven
- Want more leads, more referrals, more market presence
- Motivated by becoming the #1 broker in their area
- Age 25–60, mostly male but growing female segment
- Already producing but want to look the part

**Secondary (future):** Mortgage brokerages
- Multiple agents under one brand
- Need a flagship site and brand standards for their team

## Positioning

Meridian is the only agency in North America that works exclusively with mortgage professionals.

That exclusivity is the product. We do not serve restaurants, law firms, or e-commerce brands. We know the mortgage industry — the compliance environment, the referral culture, the borrower psychology, the language of big numbers. Clients do not need to explain their world to us.

**Elevator pitch:**
We turn mortgage brokers into the #1 broker in their market — through brand, web design, and lead generation.

**What to avoid saying:**
- Boutique
- Affordable
- Small studio
- We work with all kinds of businesses

**What to lean into:**
- Established
- Proven
- Exclusive
- Outcome-focused
- The standard for mortgage professionals

---

## Website Copy Principles

- Lead with outcomes, not services — what the broker becomes, not what we do
- Short sentences. Confident tone. No hedging.
- Avoid jargon and agency buzzwords
- Every section should answer: why does this matter to a mortgage broker specifically
- CTA is always action-oriented: Start Your Project, Get Started, Work With Us
